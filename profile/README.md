# SAES:RPG
<img align="right" height="75" src="https://saesrpg.uk/custom/images/site-logo.png">

Welcome to the **SAES:RPG** GitHub organisation.  
This space is used to manage **feedback, issues, and requests** across our **MTA** and **FiveM** servers.

---

## 🎮 SAES:RPG MTA
🌐 **Website:** [saesrpg.uk](https://saesrpg.uk)

### 🐞 [issues](https://github.com/saesrpg/issues)
Use this repository to **report bugs or general server issues** for MTA.  
Please include steps to reproduce, expected behaviour, and any relevant screenshots or logs.

### 🚗 [vehicle-wraps](https://github.com/saesrpg/vehicle-wraps)
Submit **vehicle wrap requests** for MTA here.  
Fork the repository, add your wraps, and submit a Pull Request (PR).

### ⚙️ [features-changes](https://saesrpg.uk/forums/forum/315-questions-suggestions/)
Propose **new features, gameplay tweaks, or balance changes** for MTA.  
Use this space for creative suggestions and system improvements to enhance the player experience.

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [organisations](https://github.com/saesrpg/organisations)
Report **gang and group-related issues** or submit **change requests** for your MTA organisation.  
This includes base edits, spawn adjustments, etc.

---

## 🎮 SAES:RPG FiveM
🌐 **Website:** [saesrpg.mp](https://saesrpg.mp)

### 🐞 [fivem-bugs](https://github.com/saesrpg/fivem-bugs)
Use this space to **report bugs, errors, or general issues** experienced on the FiveM server.  
Please include clear steps to reproduce and relevant logs or screenshots.

### ⚙️ [fivem-features-changes](https://forum.saesrpg.mp/c/feedback-and-suggestions/13/none)
Propose **suggestions, features, or content additions** specifically for FiveM.  
Share your ideas to help shape the growth of our FiveM community.

---

## 💬 Contributing
Each repository has its own **issue templates** and **guidelines**.  
Before submitting, please:
1. Check if an issue already exists for your topic.  
2. Follow the correct template and provide as much detail as possible.  
3. Keep discussions respectful and focused.
